Community and Content Policy

AiKya is for creative, respectful sharing. Do not post illegal material, sexual content involving minors, non-consensual intimate content, credible threats, targeted harassment, hate or violent extremist promotion, scams, copyright infringement, or dangerous instructions.

Use the post menu to report content. Reports need a real moderation queue, response process, age rating, and abuse contact before public release. Upload only media you own or are licensed to use.
